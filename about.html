About B-TEVTA

Establishment of the Balochistan Technical Education & Vocational Training Authority

B-TEVTA is an apex provincial body responsible for regulating, promoting and financing the Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) sector in Balochistan.

As stewards of TVET management, our mission is to ensure the effective delivery of technical and vocational education and training. We focus on accessibility, quality, efficiency and continuous improvement so that learners and educators can succeed in an evolving workforce.

Through skills development, industry-relevant training and innovation, B-TEVTA works to strengthen workforce development and create better employment and entrepreneurial opportunities for the people of Balochistan.
