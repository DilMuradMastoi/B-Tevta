Establishment of the Balochistan Technical Education & Vocational Training Authority

B-TEVTA is an apex provincial body to regulate, promote and finance Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) Sector in the Province. As stewards of TVET Management, our mission is to orchestrate the seamless delivery of technical and vocational education and training. With a keen eye on efficiency and efficacy, we navigate the complexities of educational administration to ensure that our programs are not only accessible but also of the highest quality. By fostering an environment of continuous improvement and innovation, we empower both learners and educators alike to thrive in an ever-evolving landscape. Join us as we shape the future of workforce development, one skill at a time.
